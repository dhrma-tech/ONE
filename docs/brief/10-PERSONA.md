# 10: PERSONA

Communication, English speaking, presence, networking, posting. Accent: PERSONA colour. Banner: mic + speech bubbles + connection lines.

## Sub-pages
`Speak · Daily · Connections · Compose · Ideas · X Follows · Market Intel · Presence · Library · Analytics`

### 10.1 Speak (speaking drill)
- **Record in-browser** with the MediaRecorder API (works in Chrome/Edge and Safari on iOS; test both). 2-minute timer with a visible countdown.
- A random topic generator from a fixed list (about 100 prompts: "Explain your best project to an interviewer", "Why Python for AI?", "Describe your college", …). No AI.
- After recording: Dharma enters **restarts** (sentence restarts), **fillers** (rough count) and **Marathi/Hindi switches** (count), then self-rates pace.
- **Baseline:** the first recording is marked baseline ("Explain your best project to an interviewer"). Redo it at day 30, 60 and 90, with **side-by-side playback** against the baseline.
- Recordings live in a **private** Storage bucket.
- Delivery checklist shown on the page: pace about 130-150 wpm, voice drops at the end of statements, no restarts (rephrase and keep going), 2-second pauses OK, fillers not OK, PREP structure (Point, Reason, Example, Point).
- 90-day target banner: "5 minutes on a technical topic, no restarts, no Marathi switching, then Q&A."

### 10.2 Daily counters
- English minutes spoken aloud (narration, shadowing, recordings, conversations)
- Conversations started
- Calls answered / calls made (phone drill: answer every call within 3 rings for 14 days; make 1 call/day)
- **Extroversion ladder** rung (Week 1: a greeting + one question daily → Week 2: 3-minute conversations with classmates → Week 3: initiate with seniors/professors → Week 4: lead a club session or open a pitch)
- Big +1 buttons. Each counter feeds the review and Analytics.

### 10.3 Connections (mini contact tracker)
- Contacts: name, title, company, **tier** (A: practitioners, B: managers/recruiters, C: founders/leaders, D: educators/creators/VCs; follow only, never message), LinkedIn URL, status **Request sent → Accepted → Talked**, dates, notes.
- **Follow-up reminder 2-3 days after "Accepted"** (shows up in Today): ask one specific question about their work. **Never mention jobs or internships.**
- Daily target: about 10 people (about 6 A, 3 B, 1 C) + 3 companies (read one open role each → Market Intel). Cap: if the routine passes 30 minutes a day, cut companies first.
- Message rules (shown as a sticky card): 1-2 sentences, max 50 words, genuine curiosity, varied openings, never mention jobs/internships, respectful tone.
- Suggested follow-up questions (static list): "What does a normal week look like on your team?", "Which skill do you use daily that you didn't expect to need?", "What stack do you use for evals and RAG?", "If you were starting out today, what would you learn first?"
- **Migrate** the Notion "LinkedIn Outreach Tracker" (130+ entries; see 14). **Many URLs there are unverified**, so import them with a `verified=false` flag.
- No automation of LinkedIn itself (manual only; avoids bans).

### 10.4 Compose (posts)
- Draft LinkedIn and X posts side by side, with a character counter (X 280; LinkedIn 3,000).
- Dharma's post rules (show as a hint): LinkedIn = professional, concise, a hook at the start (not forced), **no hashtags**; X = specific things that clicked, not "day N of learning".
- **Publish via Buffer** (free plan: up to 3 channels, 10 queued posts per channel that refill as they publish, API access with 1 key; **verify the current Buffer API and limits in their docs before building**). Connect X + LinkedIn channels in Buffer.
- **Fallbacks** (always available):
  - X: open `https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?text=<encoded>` (prefilled).
  - LinkedIn: its share URL only accepts a link, not text, so **copy the post to the clipboard and open LinkedIn's post composer**, with a toast: "Copied. Paste and post."
- Log every post: platform, status draft/queued/published, published date, URL (Dharma can paste it).
- Targets (editable): 2 LinkedIn posts/week, 3-5 X posts/week, 1 article every 2 weeks.

### 10.5 Ideas bank
- Ideas from anywhere ("Turn into post" on CAREER notes, the reading takeaways, the problem log).
- Flywheel: **Block 2 notes → X post → LinkedIn post → article**. Show an idea's stage.

### 10.6 X Follows
- **Migrate** the Notion "X Follow List" (handle, name, why, what to learn, first reply, date).
- Daily routine card: follow the day's accounts (if any), reply thoughtfully to one, reply to 2-3 practitioner posts. 10 minutes max.
- Every handle must be real and active. ONE just stores what Dharma adds (no scraping).

### 10.7 Market Intel
- Log job posts read and conversation answers: date, company, role, **skills required** (tags), source, notes.
- Weekly **top-skills tally** (simple counts, no AI). Target by week 4: 20 job posts + 8 conversations logged.
- Feeds CAREER decisions (which skills to prioritise).

### 10.8 Presence & style ticks
Daily checkboxes (quick, one row):
- Front/centre seat (never the corner)
- Eye contact held 3-6 sec in one conversation (target grows +1 sec/week)
- Stillness (no fidgeting, slower speech)
- Wall angels ×10
- Grooming (hair/beard sharp)
- Skincare AM (cleanser → vitamin C → moisturiser → SPF 50) and PM (cleanse → rose water optional → night cream)
- Clothes fitted + ironed

The checklist is editable. Tie-in: a "Harvey checklist" card (walk in slow, shoulders back, open stance, hands visible, voice lower and slower, endings drop, silence is a tool).

### 10.9 Library
Shared Library module. Topics: public speaking, English fluency/shadowing, storytelling, networking, LinkedIn/X writing, body language.

### 10.10 Analytics
- Contribution grid (PERSONA done-days).
- Stat cards: English minutes this week, conversations started, new connections (accepted), posts published, real conversations.
- Trend: speaking minutes per week; restarts per recording (should go down).
- Lagging in: e.g. "Posts: 0 this week. The internet won't know you exist."
